Client Profile

Builds an onboarding profile from the client's ASIC, CAS 360 and trust documents in SuiteFiles, ready for Karbon, XPM and CAS 360.

Home

1Choose the client

Pick one client, or add several that belong together (a company, its shareholders and their trusts) and read them in one go. Clients you read later are added to the same profile, not swapped for it. Reads each client's Corporate Compliance, Permanent, AML, CAS-360 and Engagement folders, the ATO registration letters in Correspondence, and the last two years of tax returns. The newest dated document wins for every detail.

    Or drop documents here from your computer (they're read alongside SuiteFiles)

      2Profile

      Check every detail before using it. Change anything that's wrong; the exports use what's on screen. Each detail shows the document it came from.

      3Linked entities

      Read the folders of related clients (shareholders, the trusts they hold shares for, directors with their own folder) to fill gaps like Director IDs and to see the whole group. Tick the folders to include, add any that are missing, then read them. Nothing outside the ticked folders is read.

        4Use it

        One spreadsheet with a tab each for Karbon (organisations and people), XPM (client and relationships) and CAS 360 (company, officers, members, trust), covering every entity you've read. The profile PDF is for the entity on screen and saves to that entity's own folder.

        Documents are read in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere except a profile PDF you choose to save to SuiteFiles under your own sign-in.
